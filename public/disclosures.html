Disclosures

WageMap is a static, client-only visualization. No salary or occupation search is sent to a backend we control; calculations run in the browser.

Datasets

DataSourceNotes
Prevailing wage levels I–IV U.S. Department of Labor, OFLC Wage Year 2026–27 ALC export + Geography lookup Hourly floors per SOC, joined to Census county-equivalents (including Connecticut planning regions). Annual OFLC values are converted at 2,080 hours/year. Not a legal prevailing wage determination.
SOC codes & titles OFLC oes_soc_occs.csv and xwalk_plus.csv (Wage Year 2026–27) Used for occupation autocomplete. Detailed O*NET codes map to parent SOC wage files.
County boundaries U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary file cb_2023_us_county_5m Generalized geometry for performance. Includes FIPS codes and NAMELSAD labels.
Basemap tiles OpenFreeMap Positron style via MapLibre GL Tile requests go from the browser to OpenFreeMap. No Mapbox token is used.
Lottery probabilities DHS estimates for the wage-weighted H-1B selection rule (Level I 15.29%, II 30.58%, III 45.87%, IV 61.16%) Illustrative only. Shown as 2027–2028 overlay. Replace when USCIS publishes actual selection rates.

Limitations

  • Informational/visualization only — not legal advice and not an official OFLC wage determination.
  • Static datasets lag newer OFLC releases.
  • This project is based on WageMap by Venu Vardhan Reddy Tekula (vchrombie) (MIT), rebuilt with 2026–27 wage data.
